# Agent Note: Strata role preset lineup, non-ceremonial standard mode, and legacy preset ID aliases

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-11-15-strata-preset-lineup-and-legacy-aliases.zh.md)

## Problem

In `strata-v1.0.0` through `strata-v1.0.2`, the [product bundle patch](../../../../packages/strata/product-bundle/cordis.patch.yml) exposed four built-in role presets (`strata`, `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer`) while disabling the upstream `standard`, `ptc`, `minimal`, and `cordis` presets (see [dual-track product versioning](2026-10-29-strata-dual-track-product-versioning.md)). Both `strata` and `strata-developer` composed the same six-stage stop-gate methodology workflow and identical toolset, creating visible duplication in settings and preset selectors while leaving users without a direct non-ceremonial coding mode equivalent to upstream `standard`.

Removing `strata` outright breaks previously saved `strata-v1.0.x` sessions whose session log header records `agentPreset: "strata"` and upgraded user profiles whose `profiles/desktop/cordis.patch.yml` retains `default: strata` or `selectedDefault: strata` — especially because `applyPatches` replaces the entire `agent-preset-registry` `config` object and drops `aliases` declared by `@strata/product-bundle`, raising `agent-preset/not-found` inside [AgentPresetRegistry](../../../../packages/preset/agent-preset-registry/src/index.ts) and clearing the default badge. Re-enabling upstream `standard` alone is also insufficient: in workspaces that carry a StrataHarness template `AGENTS.md`, upstream `standard` reads Step 0 classification and multi-stage stop-gate rules without an instruction telling the model to follow coding standards while skipping methodology ceremonies unless explicitly asked, while leaving `ptc` and `cordis` enabled clutters the default four-mode choice led by `standard`. Additionally, role presets (`strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer`) require starter project-memory files in fresh workspaces without copying `.agents/skills/` or prompting users to invoke `/init-project` manually, all active presets must load `CODING_STANDARDS.md` automatically alongside `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`, and Desktop Office conversions on cold macOS Intel hosts exceed `office-kit`'s upstream `60_000` ms default timeout.

## Decision

StrataHarness structures its built-in preset lineup as a four-mode roster led by `standard`, backed by schema-defaulted aliases, Desktop profile migration, automatic workspace scaffolding, and declarative Soft-Fork overlays inside `@strata/product-bundle`:

1. **Four-preset core roster (`standard` first and default; `minimal`, `ptc`, `cordis` disabled):** The duplicate `strata` preset definition and the `presets/ptc.patch.yml` and `presets/cordis.patch.yml` overlays are removed from `@strata/product-bundle`, exposing exactly four built-in modes through the [product bundle patch](../../../../packages/strata/product-bundle/cordis.patch.yml) and `packages/strata/product-bundle/presets/`: `standard` (`order: 1`, registry `default`), `strata-developer` (`order: 2`), `strata-analyst` (`order: 3`), and `strata-designer` (`order: 4`). `preset-minimal`, `preset-ptc`, and `preset-cordis` stay `disabled: true` in `@strata/product-bundle/cordis.patch.yml`, while the [client preset settings store](../../../../packages/client/ui-agent-preset/src/client/settings-store.ts) retains `requiresCodingTools` (`ptc`, `minimal`, `cordis`) if external profiles re-enable them. `standard` omits `bundledSkillDir` on `skill-filesystem` (keeping the 41 `Strata` methodology skills absent in non-ceremonial mode), whereas `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer` mount `bundledSkillDir: ctx.strataPaths.bundledSkillsDir` (and `strata-designer` adds `customSkillDirs: [ctx.strataPaths.designerSkillsDir]`).
2. **Legacy alias preservation, safe `defaultId` fallback, and Desktop profile migration (`AgentPresetRegistry` + `DesktopProjectManager`):** `AgentPresetRegistry.Config` exposes a validated `aliases: Record<string, string>` field (in [preset configuration](../../../../packages/preset/agent-preset-registry/src/preset.ts)) whose schema in [AgentPresetRegistry](../../../../packages/preset/agent-preset-registry/src/index.ts) defaults `aliases` to `{ strata: 'strata-developer' }`, preserving legacy alias resolution even when a user patch overrides `agent-preset-registry.config` without `aliases`. `AgentPresetRegistry` normalizes requested preset IDs through `aliases` across `defaultId`, `resolve()`, `retain()`, and `readDocument()` while emitting only canonical preset definitions from `list()`, and `defaultId` verifies that the resolved selection exists in `this.definitions` (falling back from a disabled or unknown `selectedDefault` to normalized `config.default`, and then to the first registered preset ID if needed). On the client, `LEGACY_PRESET_ALIASES` and `findPresetOption` mirror `strata -> strata-developer`; on Desktop startup, `migrateProfileSettings` inside [DesktopProjectManager](../../../../apps/desktop/src/project-manager.ts) strips legacy `default: 'strata'` and `selectedDefault: 'strata'` from `agent-preset-registry` in `profiles/desktop/cordis.patch.yml` (removing an empty `agent-preset-registry` entry or collapsing the file to `[]` while keeping other user settings and `!!js` expressions intact), migrating `1.0.x` profiles to `standard` by default.
3. **Automatic workspace scaffolding (`@strata/product-bundle/workspace-scaffold`) and `CODING_STANDARDS.md` loading:** The [workspace scaffold plugin](../../../../packages/strata/product-bundle/src/workspace-scaffold.ts) (`id: strata-workspace-scaffold`, `@strata/product-bundle/workspace-scaffold`) is mounted in `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer` strictly before `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-instructions` (and omitted from `standard`). On `agent/pre-step` before `await next()`, it resolves the session working directory via `ctx.workingDirectory.ensure(agent, signal)`, skips protected paths (empty string, filesystem root `dirname(resolved) === resolved`, `os.homedir()`, and `os.tmpdir()`, case-insensitively on `win32` and `darwin`), caches handled directories, ensures `.agents` and `docs/{adr,agents,analysis,handoff,research,specs}`, and writes missing starter files via exclusive file creation (`flag: 'wx'`, ignoring `EEXIST`) from `{{strata_root}}/.agents/skills/init-project/templates/`: `CODING_STANDARDS.md`, `.agents/CONTEXT.md`, `docs/handoff/LATEST.md`, `docs/agents/domain.md`, `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` (from `issue-tracker-local.md`), and `docs/agents/triage-labels.md`, without copying `.agents/skills/` or `AGENTS.md`. Non-abort filesystem errors (`EACCES`, `EPERM`, `EROFS`, etc.) warn via `ctx.logger.warn` without breaking the turn, while `AbortSignal` cancellations rethrow. All four active presets (`standard`, `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, `strata-designer`) configure `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-instructions` with `maxBytes: 65536` and `instructionFileCandidates: ['AGENTS.md', 'CLAUDE.md', 'CODING_STANDARDS.md']` so newly scaffolded or repository-authored `CODING_STANDARDS.md` enters step-1 model context, and `strata-developer`'s `persona.prefix` omits the manual Step-0 `/init-project` instruction.
4. **Declarative Soft-Fork preset overlays, Office tools (`dsh-v0.2.2-rc.3`), and `office-kit` timeout:** Instead of editing upstream files under `packages/bundle/web-app/presets/` or `packages/document/office-kit/`, `@strata/product-bundle` overlays `preset-standard` alongside `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer` in `packages/strata/product-bundle/presets/`. All four active presets standardize `tool-result-pruner` (`thresholdChars: 32768`, `headChars: 16384`, `tailChars: 4096`); `standard` configures a compact static `persona.prefix` honoring workspace `AGENTS.md` technical conventions, test commands, and git guardrails while skipping Step 0 classification and stop-gate prompts unless explicitly requested. Office document generation runs through Host `@deepseek-ai/dsh-skill-office` (configured in the [web-app Host patch](../../../../packages/bundle/web-app/cordis.patch.yml)) plus per-preset `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-office` (`id: tool-office`) in `standard`, `strata-developer`, and `strata-analyst`, alongside `dsh-v0.2.2-rc.3`-aligned `delegation` groups (`workflowEngine: true` and `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-subagent-control` with `interrupt: false`). Both [product bundle patch](../../../../packages/strata/product-bundle/cordis.patch.yml) and `SMOKE_PROFILE_PATCH_YAML` in [Desktop smoke-runtime](../../../../apps/desktop/scripts/smoke-runtime.ts) configure `id: office-kit` with `timeoutMs: 120000` for cold LibreOffice startup on `mac-x64`.
5. **Russian localization and card header layout:** The [Soft-Fork Russian language pack](2026-10-31-strata-russian-localization-pack.md) and `@strata/locale-ru` align localized preset names, descriptions, and `ruPluginMetadataCatalog` (including `@strata/product-bundle/workspace-scaffold`) with the `41` methodology skills in `resources/strataharness` `v1.4.6`, shorten the Russian `inUse` badge to `'По умолчанию'`, and wrap preset card headers in `ui-agent-preset` so titles remain legible beside the badge and preset ID.

## Alternatives considered

**Keep both `strata` and `strata-developer` visible in the preset selector, or expose `ptc` and `cordis` alongside the Strata modes.** Keeping both `strata` and `strata-developer` perpetuates user confusion between two identical engineering workflows, while keeping `ptc` and `cordis` enabled in the primary roster clutters selection around four focused modes led by `standard`.

**Remove `strata-developer` and keep `strata` as the single engineering mode.** Preserving `strata` breaks naming symmetry alongside `strata-analyst` and `strata-designer` and still requires an alias table for existing `strata-developer` sessions.

**Mutate historical session files on disk to rewrite `agentPreset`.** Rewriting append-only session logs violates event-sourced session durability and fails on read-only, exported, or externally shared sessions, whereas lookup-time aliasing handles every historical session without disk mutation.

**Copy `.agents/skills/` into every user workspace or rely on manual `/init-project`.** Copying 41 skill directories into every workspace duplicates bundled assets and risks overwriting project skill configurations, while manual `/init-project` leaves fresh workspaces without `.agents/CONTEXT.md` or `CODING_STANDARDS.md`; `strata-workspace-scaffold` creates only missing project-memory files idempotently on `agent/pre-step`.

**Edit upstream `packages/bundle/web-app/presets/` or `@deepseek-ai/dsh-office-kit` directly.** Modifying upstream preset patch files or raising the default `60_000` ms timeout inside `packages/document/office-kit/` widens the `strata(override)` conflict footprint on `dsh-v*` upstream merges, whereas composing overlays and `office-kit` `timeoutMs: 120000` inside `@strata/product-bundle` and `SMOKE_PROFILE_PATCH_YAML` isolates product policy cleanly.

## Consequences

- The client selector and settings roster display exactly four built-in presets in order (`standard` marked as default, `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer`) with `minimal`, `ptc`, and `cordis` disabled, no `strata` entry or creator-draft clutter, and `standard` free of bundled `Strata` skills.
- Opening a saved `strata-v1.0.x` session with `agentPreset: "strata"` resolves cleanly to `strata-developer` via `AgentPresetRegistry` (whose `aliases` schema defaults to `{ strata: 'strata-developer' }`) and `findPresetOption`; `AgentPresetRegistry.defaultId` falls back safely when `selectedDefault` names an unavailable preset, and `DesktopProjectManager` strips legacy `default: 'strata'` / `selectedDefault: 'strata'` from `profiles/desktop/cordis.patch.yml` on launch without dropping other settings.
- First `agent/pre-step` execution in `strata-developer`, `strata-analyst`, and `strata-designer` populates missing `.agents/CONTEXT.md`, `CODING_STANDARDS.md`, `docs/handoff/LATEST.md`, `docs/agents/*.md`, and `docs/*` directories before `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-instructions` indexes `['AGENTS.md', 'CLAUDE.md', 'CODING_STANDARDS.md']` across all four active presets, skipping protected OS roots and logging filesystem permission errors without failing the turn.
- Upstream `packages/bundle/web-app/presets/` and `@deepseek-ai/dsh-office-kit` remain untouched while Desktop/Web profiles and `SMOKE_PROFILE_PATCH_YAML` apply `office-kit` `timeoutMs: 120000`.

Mapping `strata` to `strata-developer` routes legacy `strata` sessions through `strata-developer`'s system prompt and tools; compatibility impact is negligible because both presets mounted equivalent tools and methodology lifecycle rules. Suppressing `AGENTS.md` stop-gates in `standard`, preserving `aliases` and fallback `defaultId`, migrating `profiles/desktop/cordis.patch.yml`, scaffolding workspaces on `agent/pre-step`, loading `CODING_STANDARDS.md`, and setting `office-kit` `timeoutMs: 120000` are verified by `@strata/product-bundle`, `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-preset-registry`, `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-instructions`, `apps/desktop/tests/project-manager.spec.ts`, and `apps/desktop/tests/smoke-runtime.spec.ts`.
